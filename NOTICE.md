# Attribution

KVServe extends the repository owner's solutions to the Deep-ML *Build a Mini LLM Inference Server* exercise. The original solutions are preserved in `archive/`, and the original commit remains in Git history.

Continuous iteration scheduling and paged KV allocation are established research ideas. See the Orca authors (OSDI 2022), the PagedAttention/vLLM authors (SOSP 2023), and the primary sources linked in `docs/theory.md`. Those systems' performance figures are not used as KVServe's results.

No pretrained checkpoint or external text dataset is distributed. Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses. This notice records provenance; it does not assert a new license over third-party exercise material.
